Returns & Exchanges

Home / Returns & Exchanges

30-Day Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you are not happy with your order, you may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet the following conditions:

  • The item must be unused, unworn, and in the same condition that you received it
  • The item must be in its original packaging with all tags attached
  • You must have the receipt or proof of purchase
  • The return request must be initiated within 30 days of delivery

Non-Returnable Items

Certain items cannot be returned, including:

  • Gift cards
  • Items marked as "Final Sale"
  • Personalized or custom-made items
  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered

How to Initiate a Return

To start a return, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our customer service at info@newluck.store with your order number and reason for return
  • Our team will provide you with a return authorization and shipping instructions
  • Pack the item securely in its original packaging
  • Ship the item to the address provided

Please do not send items back without first requesting a return authorization.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you via email of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within 5–10 business days.

Please note that original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item for a different size or color, please contact us at info@newluck.store. Exchanges are subject to availability. If the desired item is not available, we will process a refund instead.

Return Shipping Costs

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to a defective product or our shipping error. We recommend using a trackable shipping service for all returns.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us immediately at info@newluck.store with photos of the damage. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost to you.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our return policy, please don't hesitate to contact us.
